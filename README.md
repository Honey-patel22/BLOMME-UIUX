# BLOMME — Luxury Skincare Landing Page

**Responsive Product Landing Page | Individual IPS UI/UX Project**

## Project Overview
BLOMME is a fictional luxury skincare brand created for an individual UI/UX design assignment. The project presents a responsive product landing page with a refined, modern visual identity, a clear product showcase, and easy-to-use navigation.

## Objectives
- Design an attractive, responsive product landing page.
- Present the brand and skincare collection clearly.
- Use consistent typography, spacing, and colors.
- Apply Figma Auto Layout to headers, buttons, cards, and content sections.
- Build reusable Figma components.
- Adapt the design for desktop and mobile screens.

## Target Audience
The intended users are young adults, skincare enthusiasts, and beauty shoppers who value premium aesthetics, clear product information, and a straightforward browsing experience.

## Design Approach
BLOMME uses a bright blush-pink, pastel-rose, and warm-ivory palette with dark, readable text. Bold headings establish visual hierarchy, while spacious layouts and prominent product visuals communicate a luxury skincare identity. Clear call-to-action buttons guide users toward product discovery.

## Landing Page Structure
1. **Header:** Brand logo, navigation links, and call-to-action button.
2. **Hero:** Main heading, concise introduction, skincare product imagery, and primary action.
3. **Product Collection:** Product cards with names, descriptions, and actions.
4. **Features and Benefits:** At least three cards explaining the brand's key qualities.
5. **Brand Story:** Brief introduction to BLOMME's skincare philosophy.
6. **Footer:** Brand details and supporting navigation.

## Responsive UI/UX
The desktop design uses generous whitespace and multi-column content. The mobile design reorganizes content into a narrower, vertically stacked layout with touch-friendly controls. Both layouts share the same brand identity.

## Figma Design System
The assignment calls for:
- **Auto Layout** on the header, buttons, cards, and content sections.

Additional section colors used in the design include announcement pink `#EDBBCA`, story background `#FCE9ED`, and header off-white `#FFFDFC`. The visual direction deliberately avoids green and emphasizes blush, rose, ivory, and high-contrast dark typography.

## Typography System

- **Display headings and wordmark:** Playfair Display, a high-contrast serif used to give the brand an editorial, premium character.
- **Body text, navigation, labels and buttons:** DM Sans, a clean sans-serif chosen for legibility.
- **Additional font available in the CSS import:** Manrope; the current principal page styles use Playfair Display and DM Sans.
- **Hierarchy:** Large hero headlines, medium section titles, smaller product names, compact uppercase navigation and labels, and readable paragraph text.

## Layout, Spacing and Visual Language

- **Desktop design width:** 1440 px Figma frame recommended; the website content is constrained to a centered maximum width of approximately 1390 px.
- **Mobile design width:** 390 px Figma frame recommended.
- **Layout:** Two-column hero and brand story on desktop; stacked sections on mobile.
- **Product presentation:** Three product cards on desktop, a single-column stack on mobile, each with product imagery, title, description, price, and an add control.
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped rose-colored primary buttons with white labels.
- **Imagery:** Soft pink skincare editorial visuals and product mockups supporting the fictional BLOMME identity.
- **Interaction:** Hover emphasis on cards and buttons, a mobile navigation toggle, and a shopping-bag interaction in the coded prototype.

## Reusable Figma Components to Create

1. **Primary Button:** Label, background, padding, and optional arrow; create default and hover variants if desired.
2. **Product Card:** Image, status badge, product name, short description, price, and add button.
3. **Feature Tile:** Icon, short heading, and explanatory text.
4. **Navigation Item (optional):** Shared text treatment and spacing for header/footer links.

Use **Figma Auto Layout** on the header, buttons, product card internals, feature card groups, and content sections. Define the palette and type treatments as reusable Figma styles or variables. These are Figma deliverables to verify in the actual design file; the HTML/CSS prototype alone does not prove their creation.

## Design Rationale

The warm ivory base keeps the page bright and uncluttered. Rose CTAs create a clear visual focal point, while the pastel pink backgrounds establish a distinctive beauty-brand mood without overwhelming the product images. Playfair Display adds a luxury editorial tone, balanced by DM Sans for practical navigation and descriptions. Responsive stacking maintains the same identity and readable hierarchy on smaller screens.
